## Gate 1 — What time is it?

This is the first question, always, and it is not negotiable this week. Your body clock is the thing being trained.

| Hong Kong time | State of play | Default |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **00:00–07:00** | Gym shut. This is your body's subjective evening and the reason you're awake. | **Nothing. Stay in dim light.** No screens, no bright rooms, no "may as well train." Bright light now pushes your clock the wrong way. |
| **07:00–10:00** | Gym open, but this is your subjective *night*. Force output is at its floor. | **Walk, in your light window.** Not a lifting slot this week. |
| **10:00–16:00** | Gym open. Heat peak outdoors — running is off. | **Fallback gym slot.** Take it only if you know 16:00 won't happen. Same written load, no adjustment. |
| **16:00–19:00** | 🟢 **THE SLOT.** Subjective late afternoon. Your best force output of the day. | **Today's session, as written.** |
| **19:00–19:30** | Last call — in by 19:30, out by 20:15. | Session, or MVS if you're tight. |
| **19:30–22:00** | Gym open. Heat has dropped, so running is back on. | **Run day → Bowen Road.** Otherwise Wind-Down; lifting this late works against the clock job. |
| **22:00–24:00** | — | **Wind-Down only.** You're trying to move bedtime *earlier*. Nothing else is worth it. |

The light window moves earlier each day — 10:00 today, 09:00 Tue, 08:00 Wed, 07:00 Thu, then normal. Full reasoning in the block file; the short version is that light taken too early pushes an eastward shift backwards.

## Gate 4 — How do you feel?

**This week, your state decides *whether*, not *how heavy*.**

The Recovery Gauge is suspended until Sep 5, because a gauge score taken mid-eastward-shift measures your circadian phase and not your recovery. So there is no 10% or 15% load drop available this week — the written weight is already conservative enough to absorb a bad day.

| State | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| **Fine or neutral** | Session as written. |
| **Rough — flat, heavy, unmotivated** | Session as written, or MVS. **Do not reduce the load** — it's already 50–70% of a May estimate. Reduce the *volume* instead. |
| **Wrecked — can't face it** | **Wind-Down replaces the session.** Logged as a completed session, not a gap. |

The one genuine stop rule: **sharp pain, or shins and calves complaining the day after a run.** Then the next run becomes a walk of the same duration, and that counts as done.

## Home workout — when the gym isn't happening at all

No equipment, 25 minutes, works in any room.

| Exercise | Sets × Reps |
| --- | --- |
| Bulgarian split squat, back foot on a chair | 3 × 10 per leg |
| Push-up, feet elevated if easy | 3 × 12–15 |
| Single-leg RDL, bodyweight, slow | 3 × 10 per leg |
| Pike push-up *(your OHP pattern)* | 3 × 8 |
| Single-leg calf raise off a step | 3 × 15 per leg |
| Side plank | 3 × 30 s per side |

**The free bar rule still stands:** every bar you pass in a day — playground, scaffolding, bus stop — is one dead hang. Not a set, not a session. Hang until it's uncomfortable, let go, keep walking. Pull-ups remain your biggest structural gap and this is the cheapest work available on it.
