## Bail-Out: Wind-Down Session (the "do this instead" option)

When it's too late, you're wiped, or your head's not right and even MVS isn't happening — **do the wind-down instead of skipping.** It *replaces* the designated session. 30 min of breathing, mobility, and stretching, no equipment, finishes you calm. Doing it = you stayed in routine.

Full sequence: [`wind-down-bailout.md`](./wind-down-bailout.md).

Order of preference on a bad day: full session → MVS → **wind-down bail-out** → (only then) nothing.

**In this block it is the nightly default, not a bail-out** — use it as the sleep anchor, especially on days you didn't train.

## Recovery Gauge Protocol

**SUSPENDED until 2026-09-05** — see the Re-Entry Block. A gauge score taken during an eastward shift measures your circadian phase, not your recovery, and this block replaces it with loads and times fixed in advance.

**Resumes Sep 5.** Applies to every lifting session. Exempt: Namban Rengo.

Rate yourself 1–5 when you walk in, before you touch a bar.

| Score | What it means | What you do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **1** | Wrecked. Poor sleep, DOMS, mood flat, motivation absent. | Go to MVS only. Drop all working weights 15%. Cap RPE 6 on every set. No new PRs. If you can't hit MVS at RPE 6, go home — it counts as a session attempted. |
| **2** | Beat up but functional. Sleep was mediocre, legs/shoulders heavy, motivation low. | MVS + one optional accessory max. Drop working weights 10%. Cap RPE 7. No top sets. |
| **3** | Neutral. Slept okay, no specific soreness, no spark. The default. | Programmed session as written. Prescribed weights, prescribed reps. RPE ceiling as written per exercise. |
| **4** | Good. Slept well, moving clean in warm-up, mind is on it. | Programmed session + optional top set: add 1 rep or 2.5 kg to one main lift if the working sets felt like RPE 6 or below. One upgrade only. |
| **5** | Primed. Rare. Slept 8h, body feels light, warm-up snappy, genuinely want to train. | Full session. Push top set of the primary compound to RPE 8–9. If it moves well, log it as a reference point for future load targets. Don't add volume — go harder on what's already there. |

**Rules:**
- Score before the session. Don't revise it mid-session (the ego will try to).
- Log the score in the session file alongside the performance notes.
- If Thu follows a hard Namban Rengo, default to treating it as a 2 unless you actually feel like a 3+.
- A gauge of 1 or 2 two weeks in a row = flag for the coach. Recovery is leaking somewhere.
