## Why this shape

- **Thursday is the anchor** — fully free day at school, undisplaceable gym slot. The pull session lives here because pull is the highest-priority weakness.
- **Fitness24 is a 5-minute walk from Node Growth.** Tue/Wed/Fri evening windows after classes are near-zero friction.
- **3 guaranteed sessions** instead of 5 kills the "missed one → week is ruined" spiral that killed the previous programme.

## Minimum Viable Sessions (bad-day version)

If the day is rough, do the MVS. Three exercises at RPE 6+ counts as a session. Log it and move on.

- **Tue Lower MVS** — Squat 3×5, RDL 3×8, Leg Press 3×10. ~40 min.
- **Wed Push MVS** — Bench 3×5, OHP 3×8, Cable Fly 2×12. ~40 min.
- **Thu Pull MVS** — Deadlift 3×3, Pull-up 3×AMRAP, Cable Row 3×10. ~45 min.
- **Fri Full MVS** — Squat 2×5, Bench 2×5, Pendlay Row 2×8. ~35 min.
