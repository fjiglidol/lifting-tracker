# Sweden Reset Block — 2026-08-17 → 2026-08-29

**13 days. Sister's baby shower trip.**
**Goal: rebuild the inputs, not the numbers.** Sleep, food, aerobic base, joints. Lifting is maintenance only — no PRs attempted, none expected.

Gym access uncertain. Every strength session below has a **gym version** and a **no-gym version**. Pick on the day. Neither is the "real" one.

---

## Why this block exists

Tokyo in August is a swamp — running there is survival, not training. Sweden in late August is 18–22 °C, dry, long light. You have a junior middle-distance chassis (800/1500/3000/5k, ages 12–14) sitting under a year of barbell work and a cardio base you've flagged as your weakest system for months.

This is the cheapest window you'll get all year to fix it. So the block is **aerobic-led, lifting-supported.** That's the inversion. It's deliberate, it's temporary, and it ends 2026-08-29.

Strength does not decay in 13 days. Two short full-body sessions a week holds everything you've built. Aerobic base, on the other hand, moves *fast* when you're starting low — you'll feel a difference by day 10.

## Running Rules — read this before the first run

You have not run consistently in months and your calves are a known weak point. **Shin splints are the only real injury risk in this block.** The rules exist to avoid them:

1. **Every run is conversational.** If you can't speak a full sentence, you're going too fast. There are no hard runs in this block. Not one.
2. **Talk test > pace > watch.** Don't look at pace. You will be slower than your ego wants and it does not matter.
3. **If shins or calves ache the day after a run — cut the next run, don't push through.** Walk instead. This is not the same as skipping.
4. **Grass, gravel, or trail over asphalt** wherever Sweden offers it. It usually does.
5. **Never two run days back to back** in this block. The schedule already respects that.
6. Strides on Aug 23 are 20 seconds of *smooth fast*, not sprinting. Walk back to full recovery. This is a neuromuscular reminder, not a workout.

**Recovery Gauge applies to runs in this block.** Gauge 1–2 → downgrade to a walk of the same duration. That counts.

Sweden has **utegym** (outdoor gyms) in most municipal parks, and playgrounds have bars. Pull-ups are your biggest structural gap. Every bar you pass in 13 days is a free set of dead hangs or negatives. Take them opportunistically — they don't need to be in a session.

## Food — the trip protocol

**No cut. No calorie counting. No MacroFactor.** You're at a family event in another country. Counting is the wrong tool here and would make the trip worse.

**One number: protein floor, 150 g/day minimum.** That's it. That's the whole nutrition plan.

### Why this is easy in Sweden specifically
Swedish supermarkets (ICA, Coop, Willys) are unusually protein-dense compared to Japan. Stock the fridge on day one:

- **Kvarg / Kesella** — quark, ~11 g protein per 100 g. A 500 g tub is 55 g of protein for pocket change. This is the single best item in the store.
- **Skyr** — ~11 g/100 g.
- **Cottage cheese (keso)** — ~12 g/100 g.
- **Knäckebröd** (crispbread) + eggs — the default breakfast.
- **Smoked/cured salmon, mackerel in tin, sill** — cheap, everywhere.
- **Kycklingfilé** (chicken breast), **köttbullar** (yes, meatballs count).
- **Proteinpudding / protein drinks** in every corner shop.

**Practical rule:** hit ~40 g protein at breakfast and the rest of the day takes care of itself.

### Baby shower / fika days
Eat the cake. Eat the semla, the kanelbulle, the whole spread. Hit protein that day and it's a success. One rule only: **don't skip meals in advance to "save room"** — that's the pattern that turns a good day into a bad one.

Swedish **fika** is a genuine social institution and you're there for family. Participate in it. This is not a compromise of the plan; it is the plan.

## Sleep — the actual reason this block will work

Japan (UTC+9) → Sweden (UTC+2) is a **7-hour backward shift**. Westward is the easier direction — your body wants to delay, and delaying is what you're asking it to do.

### Arrival (Aug 17–19)
1. **Set your watch to Swedish time on the plane.** Eat and sleep on Swedish time from that moment, not on arrival.
2. **Get bright outdoor light in the Swedish late afternoon / early evening** — 17:00–20:30. Sunset is around 20:15 in late August, so it's available. This pushes your clock later, which is the direction you need.
3. **Avoid bright light in the first two Swedish mornings** (before ~09:00). Sunglasses if you're out early. Morning light would fight the shift.
4. **Caffeine: local morning only, nothing after 12:00 local** for the first three days.
5. **Do not nap longer than 20 minutes** on arrival day. If you're wrecked, walk instead — outside, in the light.

Expect to be functional by day 3 and fully aligned by day 4–5.

### Every night, whole trip
- **Wind-Down Bail-Out** ([`wind-down-bailout.md`](./wind-down-bailout.md)) — 1–2 h before sleep. In this block it's not a bail-out, it's the **nightly default**. Use it as the sleep anchor, especially on days you didn't train.
- Consistent lights-out. Sweden gets properly dark in August, unlike June — use it.
- Target 8 h in bed. You are on holiday. This is the one place the trip gives you something Tokyo doesn't.

### The return (start Aug 27)
Eastward is the hard direction. From **Aug 27**, shift bedtime **1 hour earlier each night** and get bright light immediately on waking. It won't fix it, but it turns a 5-day recovery into a 2-day one.

## Free health levers while you're there

- **Walk 8–10k steps daily.** Swedish towns are built for it. This is the highest-value thing in the entire block and it costs nothing.
- **Cold water.** Lakes and coast sit around 17–19 °C in late August. If there's water near you, get in it. Good for mood, good for the trip, and you'll never have easier access.
- **Bastu (sauna)** if it's available. Post-run or evening. Hydrate, don't stack it with a hard day, and don't do it right before a run.
- **Sunlight before 10:00** once you're time-adjusted — Sweden's light is running out fast after August and you're going back to a Tokyo autumn.

## What this block is NOT

- Not a cut. Weight may go up 0.5–1 kg from travel, salt, and carbs. That's water and food volume. Ignore the scale entirely for 13 days.
- Not a strength block. If bench or squat feel light on Aug 19, **leave them light**. The temptation to "at least progress something" is exactly what this block is designed to spend elsewhere.
- Not all-or-nothing. Miss a run, miss a session, and the block is still intact. The floor is: **walk daily, hit protein, sleep 8 h.** Everything above that is a bonus.
