## The one exception

Every other session in this block is capped at RPE 7. **This one isn't.** Work bouts run at **RPE 8–9** — the whole point of a HIIT day is the ceiling coming off for forty seconds at a time.

The trade is that it **costs a rest day, it doesn't get added on top.** Two rest days a week is still fixed. Today was one of them, so Wednesday becomes the other and the Ladder slides a week. If you'd rather keep Wednesday and rest Thursday instead, fine — but two of them stay in the week.

45 min hard cap still applies. No PRs still applies.

## The grid

Pace out a **square, roughly 15 m per side** — half a crossing. Four corners, four stations. You work at a corner, then move to the next one.

**40 s work / 20 s rest × 4 stations = one lap (4 min).**
**4 laps. 90 s between laps.** 16 min of actual work.

| Corner | Station | 40 s of |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | **Shuttle runs** | Corner to corner across the grid, hand down on the grass at each end |
| 2 | **Burpee, step-out** | Step the feet back and in — no jumping them. Chest to grass every rep |
| 3 | **Bear crawl sprint** | One side out, one side back, repeat. Hips low the whole way |
| 4 | **Squat jumps** | Land soft, reset, jump again. Not a bounce drill |

**One plyo station per lap, no more.** That's why station 2 steps out instead of jumping — the impact budget is spent on 1 and 4.

**Shin rule (still binding):** any complaint at all and station 4 becomes **speed squats**, station 1 becomes **tempo strides at 80%**. You still finish the session. You don't get to use your shins as an exit.

## Warm-up — 6 min, not optional

This is the one session where a cold start actually costs you something.

- 4 min easy jog, conversational. Nose breathing — if you can't, slow down.
- 2 × 20 **pogo hops**. Knees near-locked, bounce off the ankle, minimal ground contact.
- 2 build-up strides over one crossing, up to ~70%. Gradual — no standing-start sprints.

---

## Pacing it

Lap 1 sets the number, laps 2–4 hold it. **Count your burpees in lap 1 and match it, don't chase it.** The failure mode here is going out at 100% in lap 1 and jogging through lap 4 — that's one hard interval and three wasted ones. Four honest laps at 85% beats one hero lap.

If lap 3 falls apart, stop at 3. Three laps done properly is the session; four laps done badly is just being tired outdoors.

| Seconds | Finisher |

| **10–19** | 60 s wall sit |
| **20–29** | 30 s side plank each side |
| **30–39** | 20 broad jumps |
| **40–49** | 45 s of dead hang, any number of splits |
| **50–59** | 50 pogo hops |

## Cool-down — 5 min, also not optional

Walk until your breathing is normal. **Then** stretch, never before.

- Calf stretch, 30 s per side, against anything vertical. Back leg straight, heel down, hips forward.
- Hamstring, 30 s per side.

Weak calves are your named running risk and you just spent the day landing on them. This is the second half of the insurance premium.
