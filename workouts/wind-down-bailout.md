# Wind-Down / Bail-Out Session

**The "do this instead" option.** Reach for this when it's too late at night, you're wiped, or you just feel bad and the designated workout isn't happening. This *replaces* the session — it is not a lesser version of lifting, it's the recovery rung below MVS. Doing it counts. You stayed in routine, you down-regulated, you didn't skip into nothing.

**When to use it:**
- Recovery Gauge would be a 1 and you can't even face MVS.
- It's late and the gym window is gone but you want to close the day on something.
- Bad headspace / flat mood — sympathetic load is the problem, not muscle fatigue.

30 minutes, no equipment, bedroom-doable, finishes you calm. Best run ~1–2 hours before sleep.

---

### Block 1 — Arrival (0–5 min)
Lying on back, lights dim.
- **4-7-8 breathing × 5 cycles** — inhale nose 4, hold 7, exhale mouth 8. (Too long? 4-5-7.) The long exhale is the parasympathetic switch.
- **Body scan** — locate the tension (jaw, shoulders, low back). Don't fix it yet.

### Block 2 — Spinal Unload (5–12 min)
- Knee hugs, rock side to side — 1 min
- Supine spinal twist — 90 sec/side
- Cat-cow, slow, with breath — 8 reps
- Child's pose — 90 sec

### Block 3 — Lower Body Flow (12–20 min)
- 90/90 hip stretch — 2 min/side *(highest-value one)*
- Couch stretch — 90 sec/side
- Standing forward fold — 1 min
- Wide-leg forward fold — 1 min

### Block 4 — Upper Unwind (20–27 min)
- Doorframe pec stretch — 45 sec/side
- Thread-the-needle — 45 sec/side
- Ear-to-shoulder neck stretch — 30 sec/side
- Seated chest opener + overhead lat reach — 1 min

### Block 5 — Close-Down (27–30 min)
- **Legs up the wall — 3 min.** Eyes closed, back to 4-7-8. Falling asleep here is fine — it worked.

---

**Targets:** posterior chain + lats (pull residue), pec/front delt (push residue), hip flexors (sitting). No HR spike, nothing sympathetic — built to be safe near bedtime.

**Log it** the same as any session: date + "Wind-down bail-out (replaced [Tue/Thu/Fri] session)". It's a recorded session, not a gap.
