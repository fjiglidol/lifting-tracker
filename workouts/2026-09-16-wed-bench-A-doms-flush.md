## The stretching — what actually works, and when

Two different problems, two different tools. Worth knowing which is which:

- **Soreness** is inflammation and fluid sitting in the tissue. Static stretching does not drain it. **Muscle contraction does** — pumping blood through pushes it out. This is why a walk beats a stretch for feeling better.
- **Tightness** — actual lost range — is what static holds fix, and they work best when you're warm and *not* about to lift.

So: pumps and movement now, long holds tonight.

### Before you bench — 6 min

Nothing held longer than a beat. This is circulation, not flexibility.

1. **90/90 hip switches** × 10/side — glutes + adductors, flow through, no holding
2. **Half-kneeling hip-flexor rock** × 10 slow/side — rock forward, breathe out, rock back
3. **Bodyweight good morning** × 15 — hamstrings, pure blood flow
4. **Slow calf raises on the floor** × 20 — the single best thing for tight calves today
5. **Band pull-apart** × 20 + **wall slides** × 10 — shoulders and T-spine for the bench

### Tonight — 10 min, long holds

After dinner, before bed. Now the stretching earns it. **90 s each, breathe out into it, don't bounce.**
